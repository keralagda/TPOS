# B2C TRAVELER EXPERIENCE

## Traveler loop

`INTENT → DISCOVERY → PROFILE → PLAN → MATCH → PRICE → BOOK → TRAVEL → EXPERIENCE → MONITOR → ADAPT → LEARN`

## Discovery

Search destinations, hotels, experiences, transport, guides, circles and GEM8.

## Planning

Traveler profile, constraints, dates, budget, traveler count, interests, pace, accessibility, preferences.

## Journey

Trip, itinerary, bookings, documents, payments, tasks, alerts, disruptions, local discovery.

## Social

Discover circles from destination, hotel, experience, guide, trip, discussion, traveler or AI.

## Group travel

Discussion → intent detected → journey proposal → member interest → group formation → planner → quotes → booking.

AI may detect intent but must not book without confirmation.

## Customer account

Profile, travelers, preferences, documents, trips, bookings, payments, invoices, support, circles, saved places, stories, notifications.

## Trust

No precise live location by default. No public exposure of passports, payments or private booking details.
