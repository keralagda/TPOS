# TRAVEL MARKETPLACE + COMMERCE

## Products

Flight, Hotel, Experience, Transfer, Package, Visa Service, Insurance, Transport.

## Offer model

Separate product identity from offers, inventory, pricing, supplier cost, selling price, commission, tax, cancellation policy and availability.

## Booking model

Search → Offer → Hold → Order → Payment → Confirmation → Fulfilment.

## Pricing

Support multi-currency, taxes, commissions, discounts, promotions, supplier cost, margin and fees.

## Inventory

AVAILABLE, HELD, RESERVED, CONFIRMED, SOLD, BLOCKED, EXPIRED.

## Promotions

Campaigns, coupon rules, eligibility, date windows, usage limits, plan/tenant constraints.

## Refund/cancellation

Rules must be versioned and auditable.
