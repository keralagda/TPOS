# CORPORATE TRAVEL

## Scope

Employee traveler profiles, travel policies, approvals, cost centers, budgets, trips, expenses, invoices, reporting.

## Flow

`REQUEST → POLICY CHECK → APPROVAL → SEARCH → BOOK → DOCUMENT → TRAVEL → EXPENSE → RECONCILE`

## Policy engine

Rules for cabin, hotel, budget, advance booking, destination, traveler role and exceptions.

## Approval

Approval Engine remains foundational and reusable.

## Integration

Corporate identity, HR, accounting, payment and expense systems can connect through governed integrations.
