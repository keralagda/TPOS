# PRODUCT THESIS + CATEGORY POSITIONING

## Category

**Travel Operating System**

Travel Planet is not marketed as a collection of CRM, booking, ERP and AI screens.

It is a connected operating layer for modern travel.

## Core narrative

Travel used to be:

`SEARCH → BOOK → CONFIRM`

Travel Planet models:

`DISCOVER → CONNECT → PLAN → QUOTE → BOOK → OPERATE → TRAVEL → EXPERIENCE → REMEMBER → RECOMMEND`

## Product surfaces

### Traveler
Discovery, search, AI planning, experiences, circles, group travel, booking, documents, trip management.

### Travel business
CRM, enquiry management, quotation, booking operations, TMS, ERP, supplier, documents, finance.

### Platform operator
SaaS administration, tenants, plans, feature flags, operation modes, scheduled updates, rollovers, incidents, audit, health.

## Core differentiation

The strongest differentiator is continuity across surfaces, not the existence of any single module.

## Marketing proof moments

- Voice creates/searches travel intent.
- AI turns requirements into an itinerary.
- CRM converts intent into a quote.
- TMS turns booking into operational tasks.
- DMS turns data into travel documents.
- Social8 turns discussions into journeys.
- GEM8 expands iconic destinations into local discovery.
- SaaS Admin rolls out a new platform experience progressively.
