## H8 roles in Travel Planet

### Architecture Composer
Creates candidate architecture, modules, flows, schemas, prompt packs and product ideas.

### Architecture Verifier
Checks duplication, responsibility leakage, registry compliance, security, lifecycle integrity, naming and dependency consistency.

### Runtime
Executes approved workflows.

### Knowledge/Memory
Stores approved reusable knowledge and project decisions.

### Evidence
Connects architecture to code, database, runtime and source materials.

## Historical concepts

Historical H8 engines/protocols remain useful as conceptual references. Do not instantiate every historical name as a separate service. Consolidate where responsibility overlaps.

## Core H8 rule

> Prefer a small number of strong runtime engines governed by explicit registries over dozens of nominal engines with overlapping responsibilities.
