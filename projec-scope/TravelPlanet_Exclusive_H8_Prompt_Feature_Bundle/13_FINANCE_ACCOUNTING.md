# FINANCE / ACCOUNTING

## Purpose

Financial truth.

## Navigation

Dashboard, Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, Journals, Sales Invoices, Purchase Invoices, AR, AP, Payments, Receipts, Refunds, Credit Notes, Debit Notes, Bank Accounts, Bank Transactions, Reconciliation, Taxes, Commissions, Settlements, Customer Balances, Period Close, Reports.

## Accounting

Double-entry: debits = credits.

## Payment

`Booking → Order → Payment Intent → Gateway → Webhook → Verified → Receipt → Accounting Posting`

## Refund

`Request → Review → Cancellation Rules → Calculation → Approval → Gateway Refund → Confirmation → Accounting Adjustment`

## Reconciliation

`Import → Match → Suggest → Reconcile → Exception`

## Period close

`OPEN → REVIEW → RECONCILIATION → ADJUSTMENTS → APPROVAL → CLOSED`

## Reports

P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, revenue, cost, commission, margin, refund, cancellation, agent, partner, customer outstanding, supplier outstanding.

## Rule

Operational estimates must never masquerade as accounting truth.
