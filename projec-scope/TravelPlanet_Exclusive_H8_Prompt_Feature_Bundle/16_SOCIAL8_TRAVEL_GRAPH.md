# SOCIAL8 / TRAVEL SOCIAL GRAPH

## Purpose

Persistent social infrastructure for travelers, circles, discussions and group journeys.

## Circle

Identity, purpose, destination, interests, travel style, members, discussions, trips, events, content, rules, privacy, moderation, activity.

## Types

Destination, Interest, Activity, Traveler Style, Time-Based, Journey-Specific.

## Privacy

PUBLIC, DISCOVERABLE, PRIVATE, HIDDEN.

## Membership

FOLLOWING, MEMBER, MODERATOR, ADMIN, OWNER.

## Discussion

Posts, questions, polls, trip ideas, recommendations, hidden-gem suggestions, events, announcements.

## Journey conversion

`DISCUSSION → INTENT DETECTED → JOURNEY PROPOSAL → INTEREST → GROUP FORMATION → PLANNER → QUOTE → BOOK`

AI may identify planning intent but must not commit a booking.

## Registries

CircleRegistry, MembershipRegistry, InterestRegistry, DiscussionRegistry, EventRegistry, RecommendationRegistry, TravelerProfileRegistry, MatchRegistry, GroupTripRegistry, ModerationRegistry.
