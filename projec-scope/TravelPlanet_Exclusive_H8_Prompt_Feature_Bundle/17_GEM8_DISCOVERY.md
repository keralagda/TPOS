# GEM8 — BEYOND-THE-ICON DISCOVERY

## Purpose

Discover lesser-known places and experiences connected to iconic destinations.

Avoid absolute claims that a place is literally hidden.

## Classifications

EMERGING, LESSER-KNOWN, LOCAL_FAVOURITE, ALTERNATIVE, OFFBEAT, SEASONAL, NICHE, UNDERRATED.

## Provenance

Official tourism source, local contributor, verified partner, editorial research, traveler submission, community discussion, AI discovery candidate.

## Evidence

Source, evidence, last verified, accessibility, opening information, seasonality, interest, community mentions, editorial status, safety/restriction notes, confidence.

## Flow

`DESTINATION → DESTINATION GRAPH → PLACE GRAPH → TRAVELER INTEREST → EVIDENCE → GEM CANDIDATE → VALIDATION → REVIEW → PUBLISH`

## Experience pattern

`ICONIC PLACE → BEYOND THE ICON → NEARBY DISCOVERY → LOCAL EXPERIENCE → ALTERNATIVE ROUTE`
