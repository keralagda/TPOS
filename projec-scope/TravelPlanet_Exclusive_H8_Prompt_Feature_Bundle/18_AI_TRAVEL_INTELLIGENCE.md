## AI governance

AI must identify uncertainty and preserve provenance where relevant.

AI may:
- summarize
- classify
- recommend
- draft
- retrieve
- transform
- explain
- suggest

AI must not silently:
- change permissions
- book
- refund
- delete
- alter accounting truth
- publish consequential content
- override approval policy

## AI context

Use role, workspace, mode, tenant, customer/traveler context, permissions, data scope and locale.

## RAG

Knowledge sources must have provenance, freshness and scope. Stale or conflicting information must be surfaced rather than silently blended.
