# Travel Planet — Project Constitution

## Product definition

Travel Planet is a B2C-first, B2B2C-ready Travel Management & Directory OS for local and international travel needs.

## Product promise

Discover. Plan. Compare. Book. Manage. Travel.

## Product planes

### 1. Experience
B2C web, mobile, search, discovery, destination pages, product pages and checkout.

### 2. Commerce
Flights, hotels, packages, experiences, cars, transfers, cruises and travel services.

### 3. Directory
Destinations, places, attractions, businesses, operators, guides, experiences and services.

### 4. Operations
Bookings, trips, itineraries, documents, visa operations, transfers, disruptions, support.

### 5. Business OS
CRM, ERP, procurement, supplier management, commission, settlements, accounting.

### 6. Connectivity
APIs, webhooks, supplier connectors, feeds, sync jobs and integration health.

### 7. Intelligence
Voyage8 travel intelligence plus administrative AI assistance.

## B2C-first rule

The initial product must feel like a modern travel booking/discovery platform, not an ERP.

Enterprise complexity belongs behind the consumer experience.

## B2B2C readiness

Every relevant transaction should support:
- customer
- organization
- channel
- partner
- agent
- supplier
- source
- commission
- markup
- settlement

## Travel principles

- Journey is larger than booking.
- Traveler intent precedes inventory matching.
- Offers require freshness metadata.
- Directory identity is distinct from commercial inventory.
- Supplier-specific data must be normalized into canonical Travel Planet objects.
- Customer experience and operational truth must remain connected.
